Indian Pueblo Kitchen

Indian Pueblo Kitchen combines cultural storytelling, hospitality, and culinary experiences to connect with both local visitors and tourists. My role involved creating multimedia marketing materials that promoted restaurant offerings, seasonal events, and community engagement initiatives across print and digital channels. Through this work, I translated complex cultural and brand narratives into clear, visually engaging communications tailored to diverse audiences.

This project demonstrates how design supports marketing, education, and audience engagement through visual storytelling — how branding, typography, photography, and layout principles work together to communicate an organization's identity while maintaining consistency across multiple media formats.

Working within established brand guidelines, I developed promotional campaigns and visual assets across print and digital platforms to support seasonal offerings, events, and ongoing marketing initiatives — balancing brand consistency with audience appeal to strengthen the restaurant's marketing presence across multiple customer touchpoints.
