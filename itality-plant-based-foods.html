Itality Plant-Based Foods

Itality Plant-Based Foods needed vibrant, appetizing branding and print collateral that captured the freshness and vitality of its plant-based menu. My role involved developing a cohesive visual identity and marketing materials that communicated the brand's energy and values across menus, signage, social media, and promotional pieces.

This project demonstrates how color, typography, photography, and layout come together to build appetite appeal and a memorable brand experience. In the classroom, I use work like this to teach students how visual identity systems translate a brand's personality across every customer touchpoint.

More project images coming soon.

Through thoughtful design and consistent visual storytelling, I helped Itality present a fresh, energetic brand that resonates with health-conscious customers and stands out in a competitive food market.
