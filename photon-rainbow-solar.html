Photon Rainbow Solar

Photon Rainbow Solar sought to differentiate itself in a competitive renewable energy market by focusing on customer education rather than traditional sales tactics. The project required developing a cohesive visual experience across multiple platforms, including branding, print collateral, website design, and social media assets. My role involved creating a consistent user experience that communicated trust, professionalism, and the company's commitment to helping customers understand solar energy solutions. Through strategic design decisions, I helped translate complex technical information into clear, accessible visual communication.

This project demonstrates the interdisciplinary nature of modern design and the skills students need to succeed in creative industries. Through case studies like this, I teach how branding, graphic design, web design, user experience, and content strategy work together to solve real business challenges.

This project showcases how designers help organizations communicate complex ideas through clear and engaging visual experiences. By developing branding, web, print, and social media assets for a solar energy company, I translated technical information into user-friendly content that built trust and supported customer education.
